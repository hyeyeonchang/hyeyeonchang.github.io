I am a Research Associate in the Ann and H. J. Smead Department of Aerospace Engineering Sciences at the University of Colorado Boulder, working with Prof. Jade Morton in the Satellite Navigation and Sensing Laboratory.

My research focuses on Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS)-based remote sensing, ionospheric physics, space weather, and satellite navigation. I am particularly interested in developing innovative satellite-based sensing techniques to advance our understanding of the near-Earth space environment and improve the reliability of satellite navigation systems.

My research interests include:

- GNSS radio occultation (GNSS-RO)
- GNSS reflectometry (GNSS-R)
- Ionospheric dynamics and irregularities
- Space weather monitoring
- GNSS signal integrity and satellite navigation

I received my B.E. and Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering from the Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST).
